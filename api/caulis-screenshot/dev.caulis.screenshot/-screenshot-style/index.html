How the screenshot chooser looks: its toolbar's background, text and border, dim text (a key's name in a tip), the accent (the Capture button, the frame around a window or screen; onAccent is written on it), its corners' cornerRadius (units; half its height makes a pill), the font and its sizes, how dark the screen gets around what is chosen (shade), and its words in your language. Sizes are in desktop units.
