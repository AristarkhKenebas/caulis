Screenshots

class Screenshots

Screenshots, in the compositor itself: the screen stands still at once (nothing moves on while you choose), a toolbar at the bottom chooses a part, a window or a monitor, the pointer in it or not, and a timer. What is taken is saved (folder, as format) and put on the clipboard (copy), and a notice says so. Windows hidden from capture (hiddenFromCapture in a rule) are black boxes in every picture, and are not taken alone.

screenshots()                                   // Print, Shift+Print, Alt+Print
screenshots { folder = Path.of("/tmp"); copy = false }
Content copied to clipboard

The chooser: drag to choose a part (then drag it, or its edges and corners, to change it — or, with selection NEW, each drag chooses a new one; the arrow keys move it, with Shift by ten, with Ctrl they resize it), Tab for the next kind, Enter or a double click takes it, Ctrl+C only copies it, Escape cancels. A window or a monitor is taken with a click. Over an overview (windows drawn smaller) there is one kind more, ShotKind.WINDOWS: drag a frame over windows (Shift adds to them), click one to add or drop it, Ctrl+A takes all. Its keys, buttons and timings are chooser's, its look and words style's.

The same as actions for config.json, Lua and keys, when this module is in the compositor: screenshot (the chooser), screenshot region|window|screen|windows (the chooser starting with that kind), screenshot focused (the focused window at once), screenshot monitor (the monitor with the pointer at once).

Its settings and functions are for the compositor's thread.
