How the chooser behaves: its toolbar's buttons (in this order; ChooserButton.WINDOWS shows only over an overview), the timer's timerSteps (seconds, 0: off), its keys, how quickly it comes (appearMillis, the toolbar rising rise units: the desktop's animation "screenshot", off unless animations are on), what counts as a double click (doubleClickMillis), how near an edge of a part the pointer grabs it (grab units) and how far a drag must go to be one (minDrag units). The chooser's keys, by the names xkb gives them ("a", "Return", "Escape", "space", "Tab"), as shortcuts see them: a letter is the key that types it in your layout (on AZERTY the key of "a" is A, on Dvorak too), on a non-Latin layout the one of the first Latin layout. The tips name the first of each. (The arrow keys move a part: with Shift by ten, with Ctrl they resize it.)

Screenshots
Link copied to clipboard
class Screenshots

Screenshots, in the compositor itself: the screen stands still at once (nothing moves on while you choose), a toolbar at the bottom chooses a part, a window or a monitor, the pointer in it or not, and a timer. What is taken is saved (folder, as format) and put on the clipboard (copy), and a notice says so. Windows hidden from capture (hiddenFromCapture in a rule) are black boxes in every picture, and are not taken alone.
